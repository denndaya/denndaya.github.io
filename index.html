About

I am currently a PhD researcher in cryptography at the Hasso Plattner Institute (HPI), working under the supervision of Anja Lehmann. Before that, I completed both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Mathematics at Ulm University.

My research focuses on the provable security of cryptographic protocols, typically involving the development of security models, a formal security analysis, and the design of new constructions. My main focus is on password-based authentication protocols. I submitted my PhD thesis in January, titled Stronger Security for Password-Based Authentication Protocols.
